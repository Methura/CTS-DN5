Community Bulletin

The annual Summer Carnival returns this July with live music, food stalls, and children's activities across the central park. All residents are invited to participate and volunteer.

The council is hosting a series of free digital literacy workshops for seniors throughout August. Sessions cover smartphone basics, online banking, and video calling.

Local gardening clubs are partnering with the council to transform vacant lots into green community spaces. Sign-up sheets are available at the town hall reception desk.

New cycle lanes are being introduced along the riverside this autumn. A public consultation meeting is scheduled for the last Friday of every month at the civic centre.

The annual photography competition is open for entries. Residents can submit up to three photos on the theme "Our Neighbourhood" via the council website before end of August.

Emergency preparedness drills will be conducted in all residential zones between September and November. Participation is voluntary but strongly encouraged by the safety committee.
